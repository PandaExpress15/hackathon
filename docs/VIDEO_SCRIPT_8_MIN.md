# CareerProof AI 8-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00-0:45 - Problem and user

Students and early-career users are worried about AI, wages, education costs, and a confusing job market. CareerProof AI helps them find financially sustainable, AI-resilient careers without pretending any career is permanently AI-proof.

## 0:45-1:30 - Solution and data

Show the homepage and explain the principle: **AI interprets. Code calculates. Evidence verifies. You decide.** Briefly show the official-source coverage and name BLS, O*NET, Census ACS, BEA, and the NCES/BLS crosswalk.

## 1:30-3:00 - Build My Path success case

Enter or load the demonstration profile. Show the interpretation review, human-error checks, hard constraints, and top recommendations. Open one recommendation and explain salary, growth, education, AI resilience, confidence, and evidence.

## 3:00-4:00 - Compare Lab

Run the career battle. Explain that priorities are user-controlled and that the winner can change under a different scenario. Show where the losing career performs better.

## 4:00-4:50 - Career Universe and Skills Bridge

Open Career Universe. Select a field with a clean focus transition. Point out that planets do not spin and career moons stay stationary for accessibility. Open Skills Bridge and show transferable and missing skills.

## 4:50-5:50 - Trust and safety

Open an Evidence Passport. Show direct official values, CareerProof-derived values, source years, calculations, confidence, and limitations. Demonstrate an unsupported question and the safe refusal.

## 5:50-6:40 - Architecture

Show the architecture diagram: user input, controlled interpretation, user review, allowlisted data route, deterministic calculation, validation, evidence, and explanation. Emphasize that the AI does not invent important numbers.

## 6:40-7:20 - Failure case and limitations

Show a conflicting or impossible input. Explain how CareerProof warns the user instead of silently changing the request. Mention mixed source years and that resilience is a decision aid, not a job-loss forecast.

## 7:20-8:00 - Result and close

Return to the saved career plan. Summarize the practical value and close with: **CareerProof helps people plan a future with AI, not for AI.**
