# CareerProof AI Presentation

## Slide 1 — Career uncertainty changed

**CareerProof AI**  
Plan your future with AI. Not for AI.

Speaker note: Students are choosing careers while AI is changing tasks, wages, and opportunity. They need more than a generic quiz or a chatbot answer.

## Slide 2 — The user problem

A student may know:

- What they enjoy
- Skills they already have
- How much education they can pursue
- Where they want to live
- What salary they need

They still cannot easily answer:

> Which career fits these constraints and retains strong human and real-world advantages?

## Slide 3 — The product

CareerProof turns official career evidence into a four-step decision:

**Discover → Compare → Verify → Plan**

- Editable interpretation
- Hard feasibility gates
- Transparent ranking
- Evidence Passport
- Practical roadmap

## Slide 4 — Real official data

- 830 occupations
- 36,168 state occupation records
- 5,917 degree-to-career links
- 2,142 instructional programs
- 8 official source families

Sources: BLS, O*NET, Census, BEA, and NCES.

## Slide 5 — What the AI does

**AI interprets**

- Interests
- Skills
- Occupation names
- Geography
- Constraints
- Requested metrics

**Code calculates**

- Hard gates
- Percentiles
- Weighted scores
- Comparisons
- Purchasing power
- Confidence

## Slide 6 — Career Resilience Profile

Six transparent dimensions:

- Human trust
- Physical-world complexity
- High-stakes judgment
- Creativity and adaptation
- Credential and regulatory barrier
- Routine automation exposure

The model exposes its weights, signals, evidence, and limitations. It does not claim permanent AI-proof status.

## Slide 7 — Trust is a product feature

- Evidence Passports
- Direct versus derived labels
- Source confidence
- Decision confidence
- Recommendation challenger
- Counterfactual sensitivity
- Data-quality monitor
- Safe refusal

## Slide 8 — Success and failure cases

Success:

- Electronics, programming, law
- Bachelor’s ceiling
- Maryland
- $90,000 target
- Electrical Engineers ranks first with visible tradeoffs

Failure:

- “Which bachelor’s degree guarantees the highest salary after becoming a lawyer?”
- CareerProof refuses because the data cannot prove the causal guarantee.

## Slide 9 — Verified working prototype

- 19 workflow checks

- 0 console or page errors
- 0 pixels of mobile horizontal overflow

## Slide 10 — The outcome

CareerProof does not make the decision for the user.

It makes the decision:

- Easier to understand
- Harder to manipulate
- Possible to challenge
- Grounded in evidence

**AI interprets. Code calculates. Evidence verifies. You decide.**
