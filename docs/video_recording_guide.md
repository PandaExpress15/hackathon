# Video Recording Guide

## Target

Record a 6- to 8-minute video. The official limit is 10 minutes.

## Before recording

1. Run `python app.py`.
2. Open the app in a clean browser window.
3. Keep the bundled synthetic dataset active.
4. Clear the local audit log.
5. Test the three demo questions and the Evidence Passport verifier once.
6. Close unrelated tabs and notifications.
7. Record at 1080p when possible.
8. Keep browser zoom near 90% so the proof line, chart, and confidence card fit together.
9. Keep `docs/judge_demo_cheatsheet.md` open on a second screen or printed beside you.

## Recommended sequence

1. Title and problem
2. Target user
3. Dataset and architecture
4. Remote-skills success case
5. Internship-company success case
6. Evidence Passport verification
7. Employee-happiness refusal case
8. Data Quality and Trust Center
9. Impact, limitations, and closing

## Visual priorities

- Show the natural-language question.
- Pause on the `Verified by code` label.
- Show the chart and result table.
- Open `Inspect the evidence and calculation`.
- Point out rows used, filters, and Evidence ID.
- Re-upload the proof bundle in the Trust Center and show the reproduction result.
- Show a masked recruiter email or phone in source evidence.
- Show the refusal explanation and suggested alternatives.
- Show the quality score and cleaning ledger.

## Final check

- Video is no longer than 10 minutes.
- Audio is clear.
- The GitHub URL and project name are visible in the video description.
- No credentials, private data, unrelated browser tabs, or notifications appear.
