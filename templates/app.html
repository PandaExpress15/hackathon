KNOWN LIMITS

What this data does not prove

  • Published group estimates do not guarantee an individual's salary or hiring result.
  • Degree-field earnings are associations across all occupations, not causal returns or lawyer-specific outcomes.
  • Annual openings are projections, not live vacancies.
  • State pay rankings do not adjust for cost of living.
  • O*NET describes typical work; individual jobs vary.
SAFE REFUSAL

Ask something the data cannot answer

“What bachelor's degree should I pursue for the highest pay after becoming a lawyer?”

The system separates the supported pieces instead of pretending Census degree earnings reveal lawyer-specific outcomes.
